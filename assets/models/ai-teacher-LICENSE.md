# AI teacher asset provenance

`ai-teacher.glb` was generated with [Ara 3D Gen](https://github.com/olatunde85/3d-gen), using the project parameters in `tools/chinese-teacher-params.json`; glasses and staff badge were added with `tools/finish_chinese_teacher.py` in Blender. The generator source code is MIT-licensed (Copyright 2026 Josh Ola and Ara 3D Gen contributors). The MakeHuman mesh, skeleton, skin, hair, clothes and texture assets used by Ara are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) by Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. Ara's license states its character exports may be used in commercial and non-commercial projects without attribution.

The final GLB contains only the generator's `Idle` and `Wave` actions. The other actions, including clips derived from the CMU Motion Capture Database, were removed from this export.
